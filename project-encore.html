A ROGUELITE FOR DEAD AS DISCO

PROJECT ENCORE

Five rounds. Pick an upgrade. Survive the headliners. I want Infinite Disco to become a run you build as you play, with a new reason to come back every time Charlie falls. I also just love games like this and I take heavy inspiration from ones such as: ROR2 and Vampire Surviovrs. Dead As Discos gameplay loop is disgutingly addicting. Why not add a little pesaz?

PLAYTEST · 0.1.0-alpha.2+dev.4 NO PUBLIC RELEASE YET UPDATED 8 NOVEMBER 2026
THE RUN WHERE IT STANDS
  1. FIGHTFive rounds of Infinite Disco
  2. CHOOSEOne of three skill or stat rewards
  3. HEADLINERA boss stage every 15 rounds
  4. ENCOREStronger rewards, tougher enemies, another arena

The Idea

I love the idea of going into Infinite Disco with a simple loadout and coming out with a completely ridiculous build. Earn the game's existing skills, stack new bonuses and keep pushing until the run finally wins. Then start again with different choices.

Encore should feel at home in Dead as Disco: the music, the combat, and Charlie getting into yet another questionable arrangement with Vice. Apparently one contract wasn't enough.

How I Want a Run to Work

  1. Enter through the Dive Bar. The drums offer a choice between Encore and playing the drums normally. This menu exists as I had made the entrance, button and logic to "Enter The Encore" the day before they added the ability to play the drums :D Charlie and Vice have their conversation, then Charlie signs. Yes yes the dialogue isnt my finest work, I am not a writer.
  2. Fight five rounds. At each five-round break, the arena stays clear while you pick one of three rewards.
  3. Face the headliners. Round 15 adds one boss after the regular wave. Round 30 adds two, round 45 adds three, and the count keeps increasing. Well until a certain point, Im not sure what I am going to do in that regard, possbily waves of bosses? I am not sure yet. Groups can repeat a boss or mix different bosses.
  4. Take the boss reward and change stage. A Charlie or Vice quip appears on the same kind of animated card as the game's wave number. You know, the lil "Elite Round 15", I thought adding this would give it a little charm :D After the reward choice, the arena changes and the next stretch has tougher enemies.
  5. Keep your build for the run. Upgrades, skills and music carry on between arenas. Death ends that build; the next run starts fresh. So build your glass canon or youre immovable object, it wont matter if the disco ends!

The current arena cycle is Disco Floor, Bubblepop, Astral Waters and Subcity Crypt. Each boss clear currently adds 25% effective enemy durability and 15% base enemy damage. Those are starting balance values, and I expect them to change with testing. I am not very good at balancing off the get go!

Skills, Stats and Stronger Boss Rewards

Normal choices mix existing combat skills, such as Drumstick Toss, Heavy Kick and Windmill, with run-only bonuses such as extra health and damage.

Main skills are a rare normal roll: each normal menu currently has a 10% chance of offering an eligible one. That pool includes Fever Rush, Bass Invader, Singularity, Show Stopper, Thunderstruck and Phantom Dance.

Boss rewards should feel worth the fight. The current mixed pool guarantees at least one available main-skill choice when there's room for it, alongside bonuses such as +25% base damage, two extra maximum hearts or 15% base damage resistance. Choosing a stat card is still allowed; the main skill is a guaranteed option.

Normal damage rewards currently add 10% of the captured base value, so two add 20%. Resistance is capped at 60%. Faster movement and attack speed are part of the plan, but attack-speed upgrades aren't connected and the movement reward is withheld when its effect is unavailable.

What I Have So Far

A PLAYABLE PROTOTYPE, STILL IN TESTING

The playtest build is 0.1.0-alpha.2+dev.4 on CL-35029. The round 15 arena swap worked in testing. Bosses fight one at a time, and completed runs have their own playtest board.

Seen Working In-Game

  • Main-menu entry, the Encore / Play Drums choice, Charlie and Vice's dialogue, the stock signing sequence and automatic Infinite Disco startup.
  • Five-round reward breaks, later mouse selections and existing combat-skill grants.
  • Damage upgrades with a verified live damage-multiplier change; individual hit damage still needs measurement.
  • A round-15 boss spawning, its defeat being recognised and a stronger boss reward being granted.
  • The latest test accepted the arena change and the stage visibly changed behind the reward menu.

Being Fixed or Checked Next

  • Arena handoff: the reward screen is still stuck on “Changing arena…” after the visible swap. Resuming the next round is not working reliably yet.
  • Boss quips: the stock wave-card presentation is connected, but the latest test rejected its label check, so the line did not appear.
  • Reward presentation: the cards use Infinite Disco's angled shapes, neon frame and font. Smaller text and an empty lower area prepare them for custom icons.
  • Longer runs: repeated arena changes, two- and three-boss stages, save restoration, controller use and a real completed run reaching the community board still need full live checks.

Automated checks help catch broken state changes and API assumptions. They don't replace playing through the whole thing. That's the stage I'm at.

The Music Keeps Going

I don't want an Encore run to end because one song finished. Startup builds a shuffled, looping playlist from the available base-game tracks and valid imported songs. The run continues when the track changes, with no manual single-song setup.

Charlie, Vice and the Contract

The entrance already uses the game's dialogue panel, portraits, vocal noises (Vice just makes strange noises I will be honest) and hold-to-sign prompt. It follows the normal interaction and dialogue controls for mouse, keyboard and controller. Its basically the start of the tutorial, but I thought it was cool :D

I'm also building an original transition in Unreal Engine 5.7.4 to play after Charlie signs Vice's contract. The idea is a short camera move, a contract glow, a drum hit and a flash into Encore. I like the energy of the game's cinematic transitions; this one should have its own identity. Again, as megamind once said "Presentation!"

The sequence has a camera and lighting blockout, Charlie's model and animations, the contract and drum assets in the editor. Replacing the remaining placeholders, lining up the performance and connecting the finished shot to the game are still ahead. The full custom cinematic isn't playing in the current prototype.

A Fresh Build for Every Run

Encore should earn its skills during the run, rather than inherit a finished story loadout. The temporary-save system creates a separate playthrough with the tutorial flags set and records your original slot before switching.

It's designed to restore the original slot and remove only the temporary run when you die or leave, with recovery after a crash. End-to-end restoration still needs live validation. Music, settings and the game's shared GlobalProgress remain shared; this doesn't isolate every piece of save data. Keep a backup of normal saves during testing.

A Shared Encore Leaderboard

I want the same scores available in-game and on the wiki. The community service is deployed on Cloudflare Workers with a D1 database. It uses the already packed steam goodies to read your steam ID and username and post your results! I will make a "telematry" setting incase people dont want this. But this is just what the base game does :D Encore has an in-game score menu using the game's high-score rows, plus local records and background submission.

It uses the player's Steam display name and stable Steam ID. Scores count completed rounds, not the round you die in, and keep a personal best for each account in each ruleset and game build. These are self-reported community scores, separate from the game's official boards. Gameplay and Steam identity aren't verified by the server.

The service has been checked independently, but submission and display of a real completed run still need a full live test. You can view the same community scores here on the wiki. Online submission can be disabled in Encore's leaderboard settings file.

What I'm Planning Next

  1. Finish the run loop: clear the boss, show the quip, choose a reward, change arena and reliably start the next round.
  2. Prove longer runs: test escalating boss groups and tune enemy difficulty against the builds players can actually make.
  3. Finish the presentation: custom reward icons, clearer card text and Unreal Widget Blueprint menus, while keeping the original controls.
  4. Complete the entrance: finish the custom contract-to-arena cinematic and its sound and timing.
  5. Verify saves and scores: complete death, exit and crash-recovery checks, then test real community submissions and add the wiki board.
  6. Expand the builds: connect more stat bonuses, including movement and attack speed, then explore custom items once the core run is solid.

Lua currently owns the run rules and balancing; the native bridge handles the game APIs that need it. I want the menus built as proper Unreal assets over time, and any further performance changes should follow what we measure in-game.

The order can change as testing finds things. I'm aiming for a complete, repeatable run before putting a release date on it.
